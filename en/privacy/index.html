Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

DropMorph is a file conversion app by Gökalp Hizmetleri that runs entirely on your computer.

Data collection

DropMorph does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app does not connect to the internet and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking components. No account is required.

Your files

Files you convert are processed only on your own computer and are never sent to any server. Temporary files created during a conversion are deleted when it finishes.

Keyboard and mouse

To open the format wheel during a drag, DropMorph detects whether the left mouse button is held and whether Shift, Ctrl, Alt, Caps Lock, Esc or the Ctrl+Shift+D shortcut is pressed while dragging. Typed text and other keystrokes are not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Stored on your computer

Only your app settings (such as theme and shortcut keys) and a troubleshooting log are stored in the app’s folder on your computer. They never leave your computer.

Microsoft Office

If Microsoft Office is installed, DropMorph may use Word, Excel and PowerPoint in the background to convert documents with their exact layout. This also happens only on your computer.
